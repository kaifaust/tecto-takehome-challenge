- Calculating and withholding all required federal, state, provincial, local, and foreign income taxes

Provider guarantees full compliance with all Applicable Laws relating to payroll tax calculation, withholding, remittance, and reporting in all Covered Countries. (i) Reimburse Client for all penalties, fines, and interest charges within thirty (30) days of written notice;

(ii) Correct the error at no additional cost to Client;

(iii) Communicate directly with the relevant tax authority to resolve the matter;

(iv) Provide written documentation of the error, root cause analysis, and corrective actions taken; and

## ARTICLE 3: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS

**3.1 Service Level Commitments.**

Provider commits to the service levels specified in Schedule B attached hereto and incorporated by reference. Key SLAs include:

**(a) Payroll Accuracy Guarantee:**
- 99.95% payroll accuracy rate measured as percentage of error-free employee payments per pay period
- For purposes of this SLA, an "error" is defined as any of the following: (i) incorrect gross wages, (ii) incorrect net pay exceeding $10.00, (iii) incorrect tax withholding exceeding $5.00, (iv) incorrect deductions exceeding $5.00, (v) missed payments, or (vi) duplicate payments

**(b) Critical Error Correction:**
- For payroll errors resulting in incorrect employee payment amounts exceeding $500.00, Provider shall correct the error and issue corrective payment to affected employee(s) by end of same Business Day if error discovered before 2:00 PM ET, or by 12:00 PM ET next Business Day if error discovered after 2:00 PM ET
- For payroll errors resulting in incorrect employee payment amounts between $100.00 and $500.00, corrective payment within one (1) Business Day
- For payroll errors resulting in incorrect employee payment amounts less than $100.00, corrective payment with next regular payroll or within five (5) Business Days, whichever is sooner

**(c) Tax Filing Compliance:**
- 100% on-time filing of all payroll tax returns and remittances to government authorities
- Zero tolerance for late filings resulting in penalties or interest due to Provider error

**(d) Employee Self-Service Portal Uptime:**
- 99.5% uptime during any calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows
- Uptime measured as percentage of minutes per month that portal is accessible and functional
- Scheduled maintenance windows (Saturday 11:00 PM to Sunday 5:00 AM ET) excluded from uptime calculation

**(e) System Response Time:**
- Average system response time of less than 2 seconds for 95% of user transactions during normal business hours (7:00 AM to 7:00 PM ET)
- Measured from time user initiates transaction until system displays response

**(f) Support Response Time:**
- Critical Issues: Initial response within 30 minutes, 24/7/365
- High Priority Issues: Initial response within 2 hours during support hours
- Medium Priority Issues: Initial response within 4 hours during support hours
- Low Priority Issues: Initial response within 1 Business Day

Issue priority definitions provided in Schedule B.

**3.2 Service Level Credits.**

If Provider fails to meet the SLAs specified in Schedule B, Client shall be entitled to service level credits as follows:

**(a) Calculation of Credits:**
- For each SLA metric, credits calculated based on variance from target performance level
- Credit amounts specified in Schedule B, ranging from 5% to 25% of monthly fees depending on severity and frequency of SLA failures
- Credits calculated monthly based on previous month's performance
- Credits applied to next month's invoice

**(b) Credit Limitations:**
- Maximum total credits in any month shall not exceed 50% of that month's recurring fees
- Credits are Client's sole and exclusive remedy for Provider's failure to meet SLAs, except in cases of material breach
- Credits do not apply to SLA failures caused by Client's actions, Client's failure to follow documented procedures, force majeure events, or third-party service failures outside Provider's reasonable control

**(c) Credit Request Process:**
- Client must request credits in writing within thirty (30) days of the month in which SLA failure occurred
- Provider shall review request and respond within ten (10) Business Days

### B.1 Payroll Accuracy SLA

**Target:** 99.95% accuracy rate per pay period

**Measurement:** Calculated as: (Total Employee Payments - Errors) / Total Employee Payments × 100%

**Error Definition:** Any of the following constitutes an error:
- Incorrect gross wages
- Incorrect net pay exceeding $10.00
- Incorrect tax withholding exceeding $5.00
- Incorrect deductions exceeding $5.00
- Missed payments
- Duplicate payments

**Service Level Credits:**
- 99.90% - 99.94%: 5% of monthly fees
- 99.80% - 99.89%: 10% of monthly fees
- 99.50% - 99.79%: 15% of monthly fees
- Below 99.50%: 25% of monthly fees

### B.2 Critical Error Correction SLA

**Error Amount > $500:**
- **Target:** Correction and payment same Business Day if discovered before 2:00 PM ET, or by 12:00 PM ET next Business Day if discovered after 2:00 PM ET
- **Service Level Credit:** $1,000 per occurrence if target missed

**Error Amount $100-$500:**
- **Target:** Correction and payment within 1 Business Day
- **Service Level Credit:** $500 per occurrence if target missed

**Error Amount < $100:**
- **Target:** Correction with next regular payroll or within 5 Business Days
- **Service Level Credit:** $250 per occurrence if target missed

### B.3 Tax Filing Compliance SLA

**Target:** 100% on-time filing of all payroll tax returns and remittances

**Measurement:** Percentage of tax filings submitted by required deadline

**Service Level Credits:**
- One late filing in a quarter: 10% of monthly fees

- Three or more late filings in a quarter: 30% of monthly fees

**Service Level Credits:**
- 99.0% - 99.4%: 5% of monthly fees

- Below 97.0%: 25% of monthly fees

### D.9 Phase 7: Dual-Run Parallel Processing (Weeks 12-23)

**Overview:**
12-week period during which Client continues processing payroll in legacy system while Provider simultaneously processes identical payroll in new System. This allows validation of new System accuracy before full cutover.

**Duration:** 12 weeks (3 months)
- Estimated start: Week 12 (early April 2026)
- Estimated end: Week 23 (late June 2026)
- Includes approximately 6-8 parallel payroll cycles (depending on pay frequency)

**Process:**

**For Each Payroll During Dual-Run:**

1. **Data Submission (Day 1-2):**
   - Client submits identical payroll data to both legacy system and Provider
   - Data includes: hours worked, new hires, terminations, salary changes, deductions changes, garnishments, etc.

2. **Parallel Processing (Day 3-5):**
   - Legacy system: Client processes payroll as normal
   - New System: Provider processes identical payroll
   - Both systems independently calculate gross-to-net pay

3. **Comparison and Reconciliation (Day 5-7):**
   - Provider generates detailed comparison report
   - Line-by-line comparison of each employee:
     - Gross wages
     - Each deduction and withholding
     - Net pay
     - Tax calculations
     - Employer liabilities
   - Variance identification and root cause analysis
   - Target variance: ≤ 0.01% between systems

4. **Issue Resolution (Day 7-8):**
   - Investigation of any discrepancies
   - System adjustments if needed
   - Documentation of differences and corrections
   - Reprocessing if necessary

5. **Approval (Day 8-9):**
   - Client reviews comparison reports
   - Client approves payroll in legacy system
   - Payroll paid via legacy system only

6. **Payment (Day 10):**
   - Legacy system pays employees (live payment)
   - New System payment files generated but not transmitted (validation only)

**Dual-Run Milestones:**

**After Payroll Cycles 1-2:**
- Initial system validation
- Process refinement
- Training reinforcement

**After Payroll Cycles 3-4:**
- Improved reconciliation variance (target < 0.1%)
- Issue resolution process validated
- User proficiency assessment

**After Payroll Cycles 5-6:**
- Target variance achieved (< 0.01%)
- All major issues resolved
- Go-live readiness assessment

**After Payroll Cycles 7-8 (if needed):**
- Final validation
- Confidence confirmation
- Go-live decision

**Dual-Run Success Criteria:**
- ✓ Achieve ≤ 0.01% variance between systems for 3 consecutive payroll cycles
- ✓ All critical and high-priority defects resolved
- ✓ Tax calculations 100% accurate
- ✓ Client team proficient in new System
- ✓ All integrations functioning correctly
- ✓ Support processes validated
- ✓ Payroll approval workflows working smoothly
- ✓ Reporting meets requirements
- ✓ Benefits administration validated
- ✓ Client stakeholders comfortable with new System

**Go/No-Go Decision:**
- Week 22: Formal go-live readiness review
- Steering committee evaluates dual-run results
- Decision to proceed with go-live or extend dual-run

## SCHEDULE E: DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (GDPR)

### E.1 Purpose and Scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Agreement and applies to Provider's processing of Personal Data (as defined below) on behalf of Client.

This DPA reflects the parties' agreement on the terms governing the processing and security of Personal Data in connection with the Services, in accordance with applicable Data Protection Laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and other applicable data protection laws.

### E.2 Definitions

For purposes of this DPA:

**"Data Protection Laws"** means all applicable laws and regulations relating to privacy and data protection, including GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Australian Privacy Act 1988, and other applicable privacy laws in the Covered Countries.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person contained in Employee Data, including names, identification numbers, location data, online identifiers, and factors specific to physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether automated or manual, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

**"Controller"** means the entity which determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data. Client is the Controller.

**"Processor"** means the entity which Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. Provider is the Processor.

**"Sub-processor"** means any Processor engaged by Provider to Process Personal Data.

**"Data Subject"** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates (i.e., Employees).

**"Supervisory Authority"** means an independent public authority established by an EU Member State or other jurisdiction to oversee compliance with Data Protection Laws.

### E.3 Roles and Responsibilities

**E.3.1** Client is the Controller of Personal Data, and Provider is the Processor acting on behalf of Client.

**E.3.2** Client shall:
- Determine the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data
- Ensure lawful basis for Processing (e.g., employment contract, legal obligation, consent where required)
- Provide Data Subjects with required privacy notices
- Obtain necessary consents from Data Subjects for Provider to Process Personal Data
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of Personal Data provided to Provider
- Respond to Data Subject requests regarding Personal Data

**E.3.3** Provider shall:
- Process Personal Data only in accordance with Client's documented instructions
- Assist Client in meeting its obligations under Data Protection Laws
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data
- Maintain confidentiality of Personal Data
- Assist Client in responding to Data Subject requests
- Notify Client of data breaches
- Delete or return Personal Data upon termination (subject to legal retention requirements)

### E.4 Processing Instructions

**E.4.1** Provider shall Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Client, except where required by applicable law. The Agreement, including this DPA, constitutes Client's complete instructions to Provider regarding Processing of Personal Data.

**E.4.2** Provider's authorized Processing activities include:
- Storing Personal Data in the System
- Processing payroll and calculating compensation
- Calculating and remitting taxes
- Administering benefits programs
- Managing employee records
- Generating reports and analytics
- Providing access to Personal Data via self-service portals
- Transmitting Personal Data to government authorities, tax agencies, benefits carriers, and other third parties as necessary to perform the Services
- Such other Processing as reasonably necessary to perform the Services

**E.4.3** If Provider believes that any instruction from Client violates Data Protection Laws, Provider shall promptly inform Client. If Client does not withdraw or modify the instruction, Provider may refuse to comply with the instruction.

### E.5 Security Measures

**E.5.1** Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as specified in Section 2.8 of the Agreement:
- Encryption of Personal Data (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit)
- Pseudonymization where appropriate
- Confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of systems
- Regular testing and evaluation of security measures
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access controls and privilege management
- Security monitoring and incident detection
- Employee training on data protection

**E.5.2** Provider shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification and ISO 27001 certification covering its Processing of Personal Data.
