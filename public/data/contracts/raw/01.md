## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** For purposes of this Agreement:

(a) "**Availability**" means the percentage of time during any calendar month that the Services are operational and accessible to Customer, calculated as: [(Total Minutes in Month - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month] × 100.

(b) "**Covered Services**" means the Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Managed Database Services as described in Exhibit A.

(c) "**Customer Data**" means all electronic data, content, and information submitted, stored, transmitted, or processed by or on behalf of Customer using the Services.

(d) "**Downtime**" means any period during which the Covered Services are unavailable, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and circumstances defined in Section 3.3 (SLA Exclusions).

(e) "**Production Environment**" means Customer's live banking workloads processing real customer transactions, as distinguished from development, testing, or staging environments.

(f) "**Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**" means the maximum acceptable period during which Customer Data may be lost due to a Service incident.

(g) "**Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**" means the target duration within which Services must be restored following an incident.

(h) "**Reserved Instances**" means compute and database resources pre-purchased by Customer for a specified term at discounted rates.

(i) "**Scheduled Maintenance**" means planned maintenance announced at least seven (7) calendar days in advance, conducted during Customer-approved maintenance windows.

## ARTICLE 3: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

**3.1 Uptime Commitment.** Provider guarantees **99.99% Availability** for the Covered Services during each calendar month of the Term ("**SLA Commitment**").

**3.2 Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet the SLA Commitment during any calendar month, Customer shall be entitled to Service Credits calculated as follows:

| Monthly Availability Percentage | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fees) |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 99.99% to 99.95% | 10% |
| 99.94% to 99.00% | 25% |
| 98.99% to 95.00% | 50% |
| Below 95.00% | 100% |

(a) **Calculation Methodology:** Availability shall be calculated separately for each Covered Service category (IaaS, PaaS, Managed Databases) based on Provider's monitoring systems and Customer's external monitoring endpoints.

(b) **Credit Application:** Service Credits shall be applied as credits against future invoices. Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for Provider's failure to meet the SLA Commitment.

(c) **Maximum Monthly Credit:** Total Service Credits in any calendar month shall not exceed 100% of the Monthly Recurring Charges for the affected Services.

(d) **Credit Request Procedure:** Customer must submit a Service Credit request to Provider within thirty (30) calendar days following the end of the month in which the SLA failure occurred. Requests must include supporting documentation demonstrating the Availability shortfall.

**3.3 SLA Exclusions.** The SLA Commitment shall not apply to unavailability resulting from:

(a) Scheduled Maintenance performed during approved maintenance windows (maximum four (4) hours per month);
(b) Customer's applications, configurations, or use of the Services;
(c) Factors outside Provider's reasonable control, including force majeure events, Internet backbone failures, or DNS infrastructure issues;
(d) Customer's failure to follow Provider's documented operational guidelines;
(e) Suspension or termination of Services due to Customer's breach of this Agreement;
(f) Cyber-attacks targeting Customer specifically (provided Provider has implemented and maintained reasonable security measures);
(g) Beta, preview, or trial services explicitly designated as non-production.

**3.4 Maintenance Windows.** Scheduled Maintenance shall occur during the following approved windows unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- **EU/UK Region:** Saturdays, 22:00-02:00 GMT
- **Singapore Region:** Sundays, 22:00-02:00 SGT

Provider shall provide at least seven (7) calendar days' advance notice of Scheduled Maintenance via email to Customer's designated contacts and through the Provider's status portal.

**3.5 Emergency Maintenance.** In the event emergency maintenance is required to address critical security vulnerabilities or prevent imminent Service failure, Provider may perform emergency maintenance with at least four (4) hours' notice when reasonably practicable, or immediately if necessary to prevent significant harm.

## ARTICLE 4: DISASTER RECOVERY AND BUSINESS CONTINUITY

**4.1 Recovery Objectives.** Provider commits to the following recovery objectives for the Covered Services:

(a) **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** Four (4) hours from declaration of a disaster event for Production Environment restoration to operational status.

(b) **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** Fifteen (15) minutes maximum data loss for transactional database systems; one (1) hour for analytical and reporting systems.

**4.2 Backup and Replication.** Provider shall:

(a) Perform automated backups of all Managed Database Services at minimum daily frequency, with transaction log backups every fifteen (15) minutes for relational databases;

(b) Maintain backup retention for minimum thirty (30) calendar days, with Customer option to extend retention up to seven (7) years for compliance purposes;

(c) Replicate all Production Environment data synchronously to a secondary data center within the same region and asynchronously to a geographically diverse region as specified by Customer;

(d) Encrypt all backups at rest using AES-256 encryption with Customer-managed encryption keys;

(e) Test backup restoration procedures quarterly and provide restoration test reports to Customer within ten (10) business days of each test.

**4.3 Disaster Recovery Testing.** The parties shall conduct joint disaster recovery exercises on a semi-annual basis. Provider shall:

(a) Coordinate with Customer at least thirty (30) calendar days in advance to schedule DR testing;
(b) Execute full failover of Customer's Production Environment to the designated disaster recovery region;
(c) Document RTO/RPO achievement and any deviations;
(d) Provide comprehensive test reports within fifteen (15) business days following each exercise;
(e) Implement corrective action plans for any identified deficiencies within forty-five (45) calendar days.

**4.4 Disaster Declaration and Response.** Provider shall:

(a) Maintain a 24/7/365 Disaster Recovery hotline for Customer;
(b) Acknowledge disaster declaration within fifteen (15) minutes;
(c) Activate disaster recovery procedures within thirty (30) minutes;
(d) Provide hourly status updates to Customer's designated crisis management team;
(e) Conduct post-incident review within five (5) business days of service restoration.

(e) **Banking Regulations:** Provider acknowledges Customer's obligations under Basel III, Dodd-Frank Act, MiFID II, and equivalent banking regulations in Customer's operating jurisdictions. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's compliance initiatives and regulatory examinations.

(f) **Cloud Security Standards:** Provider shall maintain compliance with Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM) v4.0 at Level 2 or higher.

Provider agrees to execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) in the form attached as **Exhibit C** and to implement reasonable and appropriate safeguards required by 45 CFR Parts 160, 162, and 164.

(a) Provide Customer with current copies of all compliance certifications and audit reports referenced in Section 5.1 within fifteen (15) calendar days of Customer's written request;

(b) Notify Customer in writing within five (5) business days of any material changes to compliance status, including certificate suspensions, material audit findings, or regulatory enforcement actions;

(c) Permit Customer or Customer's designated auditors to review Provider's SOC 2 reports and compliance documentation during normal business hours with at least ten (10) business days' advance notice;

(d) Remediate any material compliance deficiencies identified in audit reports within timeframes specified by the applicable standard or regulation, but in no event longer than ninety (90) calendar days.

**5.4 Security Responsibilities Matrix.** The parties' respective security responsibilities are delineated in the Shared Responsibility Matrix attached as **Exhibit D**, which distinguishes Provider's responsibilities for security "of" the cloud infrastructure from Customer's responsibilities for security "in" the cloud (including Customer's applications, data, and configurations).

**5.5 Security Controls.** Provider shall implement and maintain the following security controls:

(a) **Access Controls:**
   - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all administrative access
   - Role-based access control (RBAC) with least-privilege principles
   - Annual access reviews and immediate revocation upon employee termination
   - Segregation of duties for sensitive operations

(b) **Network Security:**
   - Network segmentation and micro-segmentation for Customer environments
   - Intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS)
   - DDoS mitigation with minimum 100 Gbps capacity
   - Web application firewall (WAF) with OWASP Top 10 protection
   - Virtual private network (VPN) connectivity options

(c) **Encryption:**
   - Encryption in transit using TLS 1.3 or higher for all data transmission
   - Encryption at rest using AES-256 for all storage systems
   - Customer-managed encryption keys through Provider's key management service or Customer's external key management system
   - Cryptographic key rotation at minimum annually

(d) **Vulnerability Management:**
   - Continuous vulnerability scanning of infrastructure components
   - Patch deployment for critical vulnerabilities within fifteen (15) calendar days
   - Patch deployment for high-severity vulnerabilities within thirty (30) calendar days
   - Annual penetration testing by independent third-party security firms
   - Provision of penetration test executive summaries to Customer

(e) **Security Monitoring:**
   - 24/7/365 Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring
   - Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system with minimum 90-day log retention
   - Real-time alerting for security incidents
   - Quarterly threat intelligence briefings to Customer

**5.6 Personnel Security.** Provider shall:

(a) Conduct background checks (including criminal records checks) on all personnel with access to Customer Data, consistent with applicable laws;

(b) Require all personnel to execute confidentiality agreements prior to accessing Customer systems or data;

(c) Provide security awareness training to all personnel at minimum annually;

(d) Require personnel to complete specialized training for handling financial services data;

(e) Immediately revoke all access credentials upon employee termination or role change.

**5.7 Physical Security.** Provider's data centers shall implement:

(a) 24/7/365 on-site security personnel;
(b) Multi-factor authentication for physical access (biometric + access card);
(c) Mantrap entries and exit lanes;
(d) Continuous video surveillance with minimum 90-day retention;
(e) Environmental controls (fire suppression, HVAC, power redundancy);
(f) Annual physical security audits by independent assessors.

## ARTICLE 6: INCIDENT MANAGEMENT AND NOTIFICATION

**6.1 Incident Classification.** Incidents shall be classified according to the following severity levels:

| Severity | Definition | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Critical (P1)** | Complete Production Environment outage or data breach affecting Customer Data | Total service unavailability, unauthorized access to Customer Data |
| **High (P2)** | Significant degradation of Production Environment or security incident | Performance degradation >50%, potential security compromise |
| **Medium (P3)** | Moderate service impact or minor security event | Isolated component failure, elevated error rates |
| **Low (P4)** | Minimal impact to services | Single user issues, cosmetic defects |

**6.2 Incident Notification Timeline.** Provider shall notify Customer of incidents according to the following timelines:

(a) **Critical (P1) Incidents:** Within **fifteen (15) minutes** of detection via:
   - Email to Customer's designated incident contacts
   - SMS/telephone call to Customer's primary technical contact
   - Automated posting to Provider's status dashboard
   - Dedicated Slack/Teams incident channel (if configured)

(b) **High (P2) Incidents:** Within **thirty (30) minutes** of detection via email and status dashboard posting.

(c) **Medium (P3) Incidents:** Within **two (2) hours** of detection via email and status dashboard.

(d) **Low (P4) Incidents:** Within **four (4) hours** of detection via email.

**6.3 Incident Response Procedures.** For Critical and High severity incidents, Provider shall:

(a) Immediately assign a dedicated incident commander;

(b) Establish a bridge line within fifteen (15) minutes with Customer's technical team;

(c) Provide status updates every thirty (30) minutes until resolution for Critical incidents, and every two (2) hours for High incidents;

(d) Maintain detailed incident timeline documenting all actions taken;

(e) Engage executive escalation as needed (VP of Operations or above for Critical incidents);

(f) Continue response efforts until complete service restoration.

**6.4 Security Incident Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer of any Security Incident involving unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction of Customer Data:

(a) **Initial Notification:** Within **one (1) hour** of becoming aware of the Security Incident, Provider shall provide preliminary notification including:
   - Nature of the Security Incident
   - Affected systems and data categories
   - Estimated scope of impact
   - Immediate containment actions taken

(b) **Detailed Notification:** Within **seventy-two (72) hours**, Provider shall provide comprehensive written notification including:
   - Complete incident timeline
   - Root cause analysis (preliminary)
   - Full scope of data potentially compromised
   - Affected customer records (if determinable)
   - Remediation actions implemented
   - Recommendations for Customer protective measures

(c) **Final Incident Report:** Within fifteen (15) calendar days, Provider shall provide a final post-incident report including root cause analysis, lessons learned, and preventive measures to avoid recurrence.

**6.5 Data Breach Obligations.** In the event of a data breach affecting Customer Data:

(a) Provider shall cooperate fully with Customer's investigation and remediation efforts;

(b) Provider shall preserve all evidence and forensic data related to the breach;

(c) Provider shall provide Customer with all information reasonably necessary to comply with data breach notification laws and regulations;

(d) Provider shall not publicly disclose the breach without Customer's prior written consent, except as required by law;

(e) Provider shall engage independent forensic investigators acceptable to Customer at Provider's expense;

(f) Provider shall bear costs of customer notification, credit monitoring services, and other remediation expenses as required by applicable law or regulation, subject to the liability limitations in Article 13.

**6.6 Post-Incident Reviews.** Within ten (10) business days following resolution of any Critical or High incident, Provider shall conduct a post-incident review meeting with Customer to discuss:

(a) Detailed incident timeline and root cause;
(b) Impact assessment;
(c) Response effectiveness;
(d) Corrective and preventive actions;
(e) Timeline for implementation of preventive measures.

Provider shall document action items with assigned owners and target completion dates. Provider shall provide monthly status updates on outstanding action items until closure.

(b) Remote audits: Up to four (4) times per calendar year;

**7.5 Audit Findings.** If an audit reveals Provider's material non-compliance with this Agreement:

(a) Provider shall, at its expense, remediate identified deficiencies within thirty (30) calendar days or such other timeframe as mutually agreed based on severity;

(b) Provider shall provide Customer with written remediation plans within ten (10) business days of receiving audit findings;

(c) Customer may conduct follow-up audits to verify remediation without limitation;

(d) If Provider fails to remediate material security deficiencies within the specified timeframe, Customer may, at its option:
   - Suspend processing of Customer Data on the affected systems;
   - Terminate this Agreement pursuant to Section 11.2 (Termination for Cause);
   - Engage third-party specialists to perform remediation at Provider's expense.

**7.6 Confidentiality of Audit Results.** Customer shall treat audit findings as Confidential Information (as defined in Article 9) and shall not disclose audit results to third parties except:

(a) To Customer's regulators as required by law;
(b) To Customer's legal counsel and professional advisors bound by confidentiality obligations;
(c) As required in legal proceedings relating to this Agreement;
(d) With Provider's prior written consent.

**7.7 Third-Party Audit Reports.** In lieu of certain Customer audits, Provider may provide current third-party audit reports (e.g., SOC 2 Type II reports) that reasonably satisfy Customer's audit objectives. Customer shall review such reports in good faith to determine whether additional audit activities are necessary.
